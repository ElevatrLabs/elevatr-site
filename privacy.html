Privacy & ad choices

Updated October 28, 2026

Your worlds stay in your browser

FlaskIt saves settings, scores, custom worlds, and imported images/audio in this browser’s local storage and IndexedDB. Imported media is processed on your device and is not uploaded by the game. Downloaded presets contain the media you chose to export. Clearing browser data removes local worlds and settings. Each website address has its own storage.

Game recordings

Recording is optional. FlaskIt captures its own game canvas and, if available and enabled, its game audio. It does not request your microphone or screen sharing. Recordings remain in the current tab until downloaded, replaced by a newly enabled recording, or released when you leave/reload the page. Files already downloaded remain on your device.

Hosting and advertising

GitHub Pages hosts this website and processes requests necessary to deliver its files. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

FlaskIt includes placements for Google advertisements. Live serving is disabled until the publisher completes Google’s web-ad activation and configures the ad units and consent message. Once enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies, device identifiers, and information such as your IP address to serve and measure ads, subject to applicable consent choices. The game waits for its configured Google consent manager before requesting live ads. See how Google uses information from partner sites and Google ad personalization choices.

When a Google consent message is configured, you can reopen it here. Until advertising is activated there are no advertising choices to change.

Questions and local data

You can remove local data using your browser’s site-data controls. To retain your custom worlds, export them before clearing data. For product questions, open an issue on FlaskIt’s GitHub repository. Avoid posting private media or personal information in public issues.
